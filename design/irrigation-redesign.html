Irrigation service FAQs

What irrigation work do you perform?
  • Installation of a brand new system, including planning.
  • Repairs of all sorts.
  • Replacements of entire systems, or just sections.
  • Leak detection and inspections.
How much does irrigation repair cost?

Repair prices vary widely, and for good reason. Whether the break is above or below ground, whether the cause is already known, and whether it sits somewhere awkward to reach all change the number.

That's why the diagnosis is free — we'd rather find out what's actually wrong than guess at a price. Request a quote and we'll get you a real one.

What's involved in a free repair consultation?

Three steps, and none of them cost anything:

  1. We call you to schedule an appointment. Not all visits require you to be there if you'd rather not be.
  2. We send a qualified technician who can assess what the repair needs.
  3. After the visit we furnish you with a price — sometimes while we're still on location.

At that point it's up to you whether we do the work. Either way, the visit was free.

How quickly can the work be done?

Sometimes immediately — work can be started, or even completed, during the initial consultation. Repairs like finding and fixing a leak may take a day or so. Most other work begins within a day or so of you accepting the quote, unless it needs specialized equipment or additional crew.
